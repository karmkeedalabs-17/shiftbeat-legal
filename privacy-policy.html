Shiftbeat — Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026 · applies to the Android app com.karmkeeda.shiftbeat

Shiftbeat does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. The app has no server to send anything to.

Data we collect

None. We operate no servers and receive nothing from your device.

Data stored on your device

The following is saved in the app's private storage on your phone and nowhere else:

  • Your rotation pattern and its start date
  • Any individual days you edit by hand, including notes you type
  • Your pay rules (hourly rate, overtime and night-differential settings, currency)
  • Your alarm settings
  • A short log of alarms that have rung, so the app can show you they worked

Network access

The app does not request the INTERNET permission. It is technically incapable of sending data anywhere.

Permissions and why each is needed

  • Notifications — to show the alarm before a shift.
  • Exact alarms (USE_EXACT_ALARM) — so the reminder fires at the minute you set. Shiftbeat is a calendar app whose core function is timed shift reminders, which is the permitted use of this permission.
  • Full-screen intent — to show the alarm over the lock screen, as a clock app does.
  • Run at startup — Android clears scheduled alarms when the phone restarts; this lets the app restore them.
  • Vibrate and wake lock — to sound the alarm and wake the screen.
  • View network connections (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) and foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE) — Shiftbeat does not ask for either. They are declared by androidx.work, a Google library that the home-screen widget depends on, and are merged into the app automatically. Shiftbeat never calls them, and because the app has no internet permission they cannot be used to send anything off the device.

Backups you create

If you tap “Save backup file”, the app writes your data to a location you choose. Where that file then travels is your decision and outside the app's control.

Children

Shiftbeat is a tool for working adults. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or using Android's “Clear storage” option, permanently removes everything it holds. There is nothing stored elsewhere for us to delete.

Changes to this policy

This policy describes the version of Shiftbeat dated at the top of this page. A future version may work differently — for example by showing advertising, which would require network access and would mean an advertising provider receives data from your device. If that happens, this policy and the Play Store Data safety section will both be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. Your schedule, pay rules and edited days would remain on your device either way.
